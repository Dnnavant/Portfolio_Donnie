About Me

From the shop floor to the server room.

I spent over eight years as a technical service specialist in the automotive coatings and protective film industry, running quality audits, training technicians, and managing technical documentation across the US and Germany. In 2024, I completed a Full-Stack Web Developer certificate through the Digital Career Institute (DCI) in Germany, and I'm now applying that same discipline — systematic troubleshooting, careful documentation, and a drive to get things right the first time — to a career in IT support and network engineering.

While studying web development, I discovered I enjoy the infrastructure side most: troubleshooting, keeping systems running, and supporting the people who use them. I'm now building on what I learned — HTML, JavaScript, Git, debugging, and how the web actually works — to focus on IT support, network engineering, and Microsoft 365 administration. I'm a fast learner, detail-oriented, and eager to be trained on the way your team does things.

I'm bilingual (English/German), hold dual US/German citizenship, and I'm comfortable working across technical and non-technical audiences — something I built directly by translating technical issues for clients and training apprentices for years.

Based in Germany — open to roles in Germany/EU and remote US.
